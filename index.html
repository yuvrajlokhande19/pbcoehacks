Innovation Tracks

Solve Real Problems Across 4 Frontiers

Choose your battleground. From edge microcontrollers to autonomous multi-agent networks, build applications that create measurable impact.

Track 01

Embedded Systems & IoT

Design real-world connected hardware systems. From ESP32 and STM32 sensor mesh networks to industrial predictive maintenance, smart energy grids, and edge AI microcontrollers.

Recommended Tech Stack:
ESP32 / ESP-IDF Raspberry Pi FreeRTOS MQTT / LoRaWAN TinyML
Hardware kits eligible for special MLH Best Hardware Hack Bounty
Track 02

Generative AI & Autonomous Agents

Harness the frontier of generative intelligence, multi-agent frameworks, autonomous workflow orchestration, and localized small language models (SLMs) running directly on edge hardware.

Recommended Tech Stack:
LangChain / LlamaIndex Ollama / Local LLMs CrewAI / AutoGen Vector Databases
Free Cloud GPU / API tokens provided for all registered teams
Track 03

Healthcare & Smart Cities

Build solutions for critical infrastructure: remote tele-health monitoring, vital signs diagnostics, intelligent traffic telematics, waste management, municipal crisis dispatch, and clean air sensing.

Recommended Tech Stack:
Bio-sensors (ECG/PPG) OpenStreetMap / GIS WebSockets FHIR / Health APIs
Civic challenge track in collaboration with municipal advisors
Track 04

Open Innovation & Social Good

Have an audacious concept that breaks the mold? Build for agritech, assistive technologies for individuals with disabilities, disaster relief, green energy, or decentralized open source public goods.

Recommended Tech Stack:
Open Source Stacks Computer Vision Audio AI PWA / Offline-First
Judged on creativity, accessibility, and real-world societal impact

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about eligibility, equipment, zero fees, and our code of conduct.

Yes, absolutely 100% free! There are zero registration fees, zero participation charges, and zero hidden costs. Thanks to our partners, all accepted participants receive complimentary admission, access to the MLH Hardware Lab, 5 meals, snacks, high-speed Wi-Fi, swag boxes, and overnight accommodation at PBCOE.
Any currently enrolled student — whether pursuing an undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma degree in engineering, technology, science, or design from any university or college across India — is welcome to apply! External university students are actively encouraged.

PBCOEHacks is an official MLH Member Event partner. All attendees, mentors, judges, volunteers, and sponsors must abide by the official Major League Hacking Code of Conduct.

We believe in providing a safe, harassment-free, welcoming, and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, or religion.

Yes! In fact, hardware is one of our flagship focus tracks. We provide access to the MLH Hardware Lab containing microcontrollers (ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi), environmental sensors, IMUs, cameras, and soldering stations. You are also welcome to bring your own specialized sensors or development boards.
Teams can consist of 2 to 4 members. You can register with a pre-formed team on Devfolio, or apply as an individual and join our pre-event Discord mixer where you can connect with other accepted hackers and form a team.
Bring your valid College/University Student ID card, government-issued photo ID, laptop, chargers, extension cords, toiletries, comfortable clothes, jacket/blanket for overnight rest zones, and any specialized hardware components you want to experiment with.
